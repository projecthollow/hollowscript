# Trademark policy

**Status, 2026-10-07:** The owner has decided to retain HollowScript and the
current domains and routing. The owner's self-search was completed on
2026-09-23; the report records an active same-field project using the similar
name HoloScript, and that project's live use was reconfirmed on 2026-10-06.
The search did not find an exact registration in the databases checked at
that time. It is not a legal clearance opinion or a current comprehensive
register search. This policy describes the project's intended use of the
name; it does not claim that the name is registered or legally cleared.

## The symbol

Use HollowScript™ where the project names itself prominently, such as a
page header, footer or the first mention in a README. Do not use ® unless
the owner verifies that a registration has been granted in the relevant
country. Other people writing about the language need not use either
symbol.

## What this covers

The name "HollowScript" and any logo or wordmark this project publishes
alongside it. It does not cover the language specification's own content
(licensed under CC BY 4.0, which already lets you copy and adapt it with
attribution) or this implementation's source code (licensed under
Apache-2.0 or MIT, at your option). A licence to the code and the
specification is not a licence to the name.

The logo's master files, and the rules for its colours, clear space,
minimum size and what not to do with it, are in
[docs/brand/](docs/brand/README.md). Those files are not licensed under
CC BY 4.0, Apache-2.0 or MIT, although they sit under `docs/`; this
policy is the only thing that governs their use. Use those files unaltered when you
refer to the project with its logo in a way this policy allows.

## What you can do without asking

- Say, factually, that your project implements HollowScript, is compatible
  with HollowScript, or is based on HollowScript, the same way you would
  name any other language or format your software works with ("a
  HollowScript interpreter", "targets the HollowScript bytecode format").
- Link to and quote the specification and this repository, with
  attribution, under their own licences.
- Refer to the language by name when writing about it, comparing it to
  other languages, or teaching it.

## What needs a different name

- **A fork or a modified implementation.** If you take this source and
  change the language it accepts, its standard library, its conformance
  behaviour, or otherwise produce something that is not this
  specification's own implementation, it needs its own name. Do not call a
  fork "HollowScript" or something a reasonable reader would mistake for an
  official build (for example "HollowScript Pro" or "HollowScriptX").
- **A product or company name.** Do not use "HollowScript" as, or as the
  leading element of, the name of a product, service or company that is not
  this project.
- **Anything implying endorsement.** Do not use the name or logo in a way
  that suggests Soulstack endorses, sponsors or is affiliated with your
  project, unless that is actually true.

## Compatibility claims stay factual

Describing what your software does in relation to HollowScript, honestly
and without implying endorsement, is always fine: "reads HollowScript
bytecode", "an editor plugin for HollowScript", "passes N of the
HollowScript conformance corpus's cases". The line is between describing a
relationship to the language and claiming to be, or be part of, the project
itself.

## Questions

If you are not sure whether a particular use is fine, ask before you ship
it; see SECURITY.md and CONTRIBUTING.md for how issues reach the
maintaining team. This is a small, early project, and the intent of this
policy is to keep the name meaningful, not to make ordinary, honest use of
it difficult.
